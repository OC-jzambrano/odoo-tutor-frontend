Hierarchy

One prominent next action per state; neutral controls for supporting tasks; restrained red for destructive actions. Navigation stays separate from capture actions.

Detailed actions

The compact ⋯ represents a proposed step-action menu for existing optional and remove controls. Selection, warnings, batch actions, undo, and dry-run controls remain visible when relevant. Menu placement needs your approval.

Scope

Recorder interface only, including light and dark themes and narrow screens. Handlers, permissions, saving, AI processing and review decisions stay unchanged. Session ID remains accessible as secondary information.

Picking and editing

When an element is picked, show its target, Clear, an optional instruction and Add step. Each step retains selection, an editable instruction, optional status, locate and remove. A menu for optional and remove is a proposed interaction change, pending approval.

Polishing and selection

Show progress while polishing and Undo after completion. Show possible extra-step warnings with Keep and Remove; show optional suggestions with their existing accept and dismiss actions. Selecting steps reveals Mark optional, Make required, Delete and Clear selection.

Testing and errors

During a test, show the current step and existing Resume test or Bubble mode and Stop controls. The existing test interface retains locate, edit, restart, back, skip and delete. Review errors retain their message, dismiss and Retry Publish controls.

Availability and access

Start remains disabled until title and module are provided. Polish, test and save remain available with steps, including during capture, subject to their existing loading conditions. Review remains limited to authorized users. Focus, disabled states and text labels remain visible.
